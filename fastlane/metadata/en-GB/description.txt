Tuji is a picture dictionary for learning Japanese and English words: the things you run into living in Japan, from the convenience store to the station, grouped by topic with pictures, audio and example sentences, and kept in memory with spaced repetition.

■ A word book you read like a picture dictionary
Every word comes with a picture, pronunciation and example sentences, grouped by topic into picture dictionaries you can simply page through. Word pages also show easily confused words.

■ Scene practice: understand what staff ask (learning Japanese)
22 scenes, from the convenience store and restaurant to the hotel, station and airport. Hear the staff, pick a reply in Japanese or work the payment or ticket machine, and see what happens. Reviews use phrasings you haven't heard, and you can check every line offline before you walk in.

■ New words in three steps
"Look and remember → pick it out → put it together", with other words mixed in between, so by the time you recall a word it has left short-term memory. That is when it sticks.

■ Spaced repetition and five mastery levels
Tuji schedules each review from how quickly and how correctly you answered. Every word has five mastery levels, and the home screen shows at a glance how many words to learn and to review today.

■ Your own word lists and notes (members)
Gather the words you want to learn into your own lists, then study and review straight from a list. Keep a note of your own on any word.

■ Snap a photo, grow your own picture dictionary (members)
Don't know what something is called? Just take a photo. AI recognizes it and makes a card — picture, meaning and example sentences — that goes straight into your study and reviews.

■ Ask Moira: AI vocabulary, grammar, stories and chat (uses Can Points)
Tap Moira on the Today screen:
• AI vocabulary: look up a word or phrase, get an in-depth analysis, or ask Moira for a memory aid.
• AI grammar: have a grammar point explained, compare similar ones, or get a practice set.
• Story time: Moira writes a short dialogue around words you're learning; read each line aloud.
• Topic chat: chat with Moira about a topic in the language you're learning, with help when you're stuck and a review at the end.

■ Sightings: see what others have collected
Anyone can browse published collections; members can save them, study them and publish their own.

■ Also
• Daily study reminders (available to everyone)
• Interface in Traditional Chinese, Simplified Chinese, Japanese and English
• Answer offline; progress syncs when you're back online
• Daily goals, pronunciation audio and a study record
