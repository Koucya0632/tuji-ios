Tuji is a Japanese study app for people going to Japan.
At a convenience store, the clerk may ask whether to heat your bento. Hear it here first, so it isn't new at the register.

■ Scene practice: understand what staff ask (learning Japanese)
22 scenes, including the convenience store, restaurant, ramen shop, hotel, station and airport. Hear the staff, pick a reply in Japanese, or work the payment or meal ticket machine and see what happens. Reviews use phrasings you haven't heard yet. Every line can be checked offline before you walk in.

■ The atlas: know what you're looking at
Every word comes with a picture, audio and example sentences, grouped by topic. The Student, Working holiday and Travel sets are a place to start. Word pages also show easily confused words.

■ Travel with Moira (learning Japanese)
Open the map of Japan from Sightings, pick a city, then a spot. Each spot lists 8 words you'll come across there, to study and review. Spots marked free, such as Akihabara and Dotonbori, are open to anyone signed in. The rest need Lifetime Membership.

■ New words in three steps, then spaced repetition
"Look and remember → pick it out → put it together", with other words mixed in between. Each review is then scheduled from how quickly and how accurately you answered. Every word has five mastery levels, and the home screen shows what's due today.

■ Snap a photo, grow your own atlas (Lifetime Membership, uses points)
Take a photo of something you can't name. AI recognizes it and makes a card with a picture, meaning and example sentences.

■ Ask Moira (Lifetime Membership, uses points)
Tap Moira on the Today screen:
• AI vocabulary: look up a word or phrase, get an in-depth analysis, or ask for a memory aid.
• AI grammar: explain a grammar point, compare similar ones, or get a practice set.
• Story time: a short dialogue around words you're learning; read each line aloud.
• Topic chat: talk about a topic in the language you're learning, with help when you're stuck and a review at the end.

■ Sightings: see what others have collected
Anyone can browse published collections. Lifetime members can save and study them, and publish their own.
