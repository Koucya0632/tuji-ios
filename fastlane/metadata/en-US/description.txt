Tuji is a picture dictionary of words that grows with you. Learn with illustrations, turn photos into cards, and lock words in with spaced repetition.

■ A word book you read like a picture dictionary
Every word comes with a picture, pronunciation and example sentences, grouped by theme into picture dictionaries you can simply page through. Word pages also show easily confused words.

■ New words in three steps
"Look and remember → pick it out → put it together" — and other words are mixed in between each step. By the time you have to recall a word, the answer has left your short-term memory. That is when it actually sticks.

■ Spaced repetition and five mastery levels
Tuji schedules each review from how quickly and how correctly you answered. Every word has five mastery levels, and the home screen shows at a glance how many words to learn and to review today.

■ Your own word lists and notes (members)
Gather the words you want to learn into your own lists, then study and review straight from a list. Keep a note of your own on any word.

■ Snap a photo, grow your own picture dictionary (members)
Don't know what something is called? Just take a photo. AI recognizes it and makes a card — picture, meaning and example sentences — that goes straight into your study and reviews.

■ 物見: see what others have collected
Anyone can browse published picture dictionaries and collections. Members can save what they like, add it to their studies, and publish their own.
